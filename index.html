Makeup tools

A few well-loved tools are all you need. Choose brushes that feel comfortable in your hand, and give them a gentle clean regularly.

The essentials

A soft complexion brush, a fluffy blending brush, and a small detail brush make a versatile starting set.

Wash weekly with a mild cleanser, reshape the bristles, and let each brush dry flat.
